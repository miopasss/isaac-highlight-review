自定义任意道具的掉落权重。

· 0　=　完全不出现
· 0.5　=　出现率减半
· 1　=　保持原样

也可以覆盖道具评级 —— 装了 Highlight Review 的话，它的「四级道具」判定会用你设的评级。

【用法】游戏内按 F6 打开，也可以在 ImGui 顶栏 Item Weight → 设置 里改键。
三个入口：搜索 / 已获得道具 / 已修改，不用背道具 id。

【注意】权重目前只能降低，不能提高（填 > 1 不生效）。
配置按存档槽保存（save1/2/3 各一份）。

需要 REPENTOGON。

Change how often any item shows up.

· 0　=　never appears
· 0.5　=　half as often
· 1　=　unchanged

Also overrides item quality — if Highlight Review is installed, its "quality 4" list uses the ratings you set here.

How to use: press F6 in game, or rebind it in the ImGui menu: Item Weight → Settings.
Three ways to find an item: search by id/name, pick from what you own, or browse what you already edited.

Note: weight can only be lowered, not raised (values above 1 are saved but do nothing).
Settings are stored per save slot (save1/2/3 are separate).

Requires REPENTOGON.
