Font attribution
================
Files in this folder:
  eid_cn_default.fnt
  eid_cn_default_0.png
  eid_cn_fix_0.png

Origin: "External Item Descriptions" (EID) mod for The Binding of Isaac: Repentance
        by wofsauge and contributors.
Mod folder: external item descriptions_836319872
Path in that mod: resources/font/
Upstream: https://github.com/wofsauge/External-Item-Descriptions

These are CJK (Chinese) bitmap fonts. They are redistributed here unmodified so that
Highlight Review can render Chinese text in its in-game book UI; the game's built-in
bitmap fonts contain no CJK glyphs. EID is free and open source.

If you are the rights holder and want this removed, please open an issue.
